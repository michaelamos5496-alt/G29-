Frequently asked questions

Questions people actually ask us

The real questions behind renting, buying and staying in Accra. Something else on your mind? Contact us or message us on WhatsApp.

I land at Kotoka late tonight. Where can I actually sleep?

Palm Court, a furnished three-bedroom apartment in Airport Residential, is minutes from the airport, so you skip the long drive across the city. Message us on WhatsApp with your flight and dates and we will confirm availability. See it on the short-stay page.

Why a villa in Aburi instead of a hotel?

Vida Villa sits in the hills above Accra with fresh air and mountain views, five bedrooms and 6.5 bathrooms, so a family or group stays under one roof instead of booking several hotel rooms. It is $500 per day. Good for weekends away, retreats and celebrations.

I do not know which part of Accra suits me. Where do I start?

Think of it by mood. Airport Residential for premium living minutes from Kotoka. Cantonments for tree-lined streets near the embassies. Osu for city-centre energy with beachside nightlife close by. East Legon for Accra's most established residential address. North Ridge for an elevated, established area close to the city centre. McCarthy Hill for townhouse living in a gated village. Or answer a few questions in our Find My Property tool and we will narrow it down.

I own a property in Accra but I am abroad. Who looks after it?

We do. Our facility management team handles upkeep and maintenance, supervises on site, finds tenants and collects rent, and sends you a monthly owner statement, so you can see what is happening without being there. Details on the facility management page.

My property feels tired. Is renovating worth it?

Often, yes, because the right refurbishment protects and raises what a property is worth, whether you plan to rent it, sell it or live in it. Tell us what you have and what you want from it, and our refurbishment and renovation team will talk it through with you.

Yes. Book a viewing from any page, or message us on WhatsApp at +233 54 993 3727, and a member of our team will arrange a time with you. Our office is at 50 Mango Tree Avenue, Accra, open Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm.
