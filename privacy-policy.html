Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

1. Introduction

G29 Property Consult ("G29", "we", "us", or "our") provides property management, brokerage, valuation, and advisory services in Accra, Ghana. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website (g29propertyconsult.com and g29.vercel.app), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this site, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information that you choose to give us. This site does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. The information we collect includes:

  • Contact and inquiry details — name, email address, phone number, and message content, submitted through our contact form, property inquiry forms, or the floating "Book a Viewing" / WhatsApp / call / email options.
  • Communications you send us directly — by phone, WhatsApp, or email, including any details you share about your property interests, budget, or timeline.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiries about listings, viewings, valuations, or advisory services;
  • Arrange property viewings and connect you with the relevant G29 advisor;
  • Follow up on requests for pricing, financing options, or additional property details;
  • Maintain records of client and prospect communications for our internal service delivery.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

This website loads a small number of third-party services needed to display content:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) — for site typography.
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) — hosts the GSAP animation library used for page motion.
  • Unsplash (images.unsplash.com) — hosts stock photography used as placeholder imagery.
  • Google Maps — embedded on individual property pages to show a listing's location.
  • WhatsApp (wa.me) — opens WhatsApp when you choose to message us that way.

These providers may process standard technical data (such as your IP address) when your browser requests content from them, under their own privacy policies. We do not control, and are not responsible for, how these third parties handle that data.

5. Data Retention & Security

We keep enquiry and contact information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request and maintain our client records, after which it is deleted or anonymized. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information you share with us, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Your Rights

Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Children's Privacy

This site and our services are intended for adults seeking property services. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
